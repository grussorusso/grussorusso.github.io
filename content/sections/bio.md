I am an Assistant Professor (RTT) at the Department of Civil Engineering and Computer Science Engineering of the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://web.uniroma2.it/en). My research focuses on distributed computing systems with emphasis on performance optimization, runtime self-adaptation, serverless computing in the edge-cloud continuum, and resource management for AI/ML workloads.
